# A smarter company every day

Five parts. That is the whole operating system.

```text
drop  →  inbox/   calls/
truth →  knowledge/   INDEX.md   _provisional.md   _graveyard.md
trail →  DECISIONS.md   ACTIVITY.md   logs/
wip   →  workspaces/
mind  →  CLAUDE.md   skills/   scripts/   hooks/
```

| Part | Job |
|------|-----|
| 1. Somewhere to drop things | Capture without filing. Speed over structure. |
| 2. Knowledge, kept honest | Facts we work from — plus doubt and disproof. |
| 3. The paper trail | Choices, activity, session summaries. |
| 4. Room for unfinished work | Personal drafts that are allowed to be messy. |
| 5. How the agent behaves | Operating instructions, repeatable skills, overnight jobs. |

Start: [`CLAUDE.md`](./CLAUDE.md) then [`knowledge/INDEX.md`](./knowledge/INDEX.md).

Product specs (what we are building) stay in [`docs/`](./docs/README.md). This OS is **how the company remembers**.
